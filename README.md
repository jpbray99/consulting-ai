# Tailored AI consulting website

Working business name: Tailored AI. Static responsive site with automation, custom in-house applications, software cost review, a savings estimator, FAQs, and a locally generated project brief.

Cartier and Skechers logos come from their official websites. The logo strip refers to prior work through LeapVision, not consulting engagements by a newly created company. Numerical outcomes are explicitly illustrative, with transparent calculations and the build-cost caveat.

Contact currently prepares a copyable brief and sends nothing. Connect the business email or booking destination once supplied. No contact data is stored or transmitted. No team section.

Serve `dist/` to preview. Sites publication settings live in `.openai/hosting.json`.

Positioning: helping SMEs move beyond basic AI questions and occasional writing to practical adoption through tailored workflows, custom apps, team training, and support.
